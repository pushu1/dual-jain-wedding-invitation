## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Entirely in English**: Designed with classical English typography (Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Inter) with respectful and accurate transliterations of traditional Indian wedding customs and rituals.
2. **Equal Prominence for Both Couples**: Displayed side-by-side with equal visual weight on the opening screen, hero invitation, wedding schedule, and family letter.
3. **Interactive Opening Screen**: Ceremonial entrance screen with traditional Lord Ganesha illustration, auspicious date, and "Enter Invitation" button that unveils the full card with music.
4. **Authentic Royal Wedding Audio**: Background shehnai & royal Indian instrumental music with seamless play/pause controls, respecting browser autoplay standards, plus a Web Audio API procedural synthesis fallback.
5. **Real-time Live Countdown Timer**: Counting down in days, hours, minutes, and seconds to December 12, 2026.
6. **9 Traditional Wedding Events (Mangalik Karyakram)**:
   - Ghritpaan Ceremony (7 Dec 2026, 3:15 PM)
   - Vinayak Ceremony (7 Dec 2026, 5:15 PM)
   - Haldi & Mehendi (8 Dec 2026, 1:15 PM)
   - Mayra Ceremony (9 Dec 2026, 12:15 PM)
   - Bandoli Ceremony (9 Dec 2026, 5:15 PM)
   - Ladies Sangeet (9 Dec 2026, 8:00 PM)
   - Departure of Baraat (10 Dec 2026, 12:15 PM)
   - Welcome of Baraat (12 Dec 2026, 5:15 PM)
   - Wedding Ceremony / Panigrahan Sanskar (12 Dec 2026, 10:15 PM)
7. **Add to Calendar (.ics)**: Download calendar event reminders with one click.
8. **Baraat Travel Notices**: Details for Samdari and Balotra bus routes.
9. **Highlighted Blessing Ceremony & Reception**: Ashirwad Samaroh & Pritibhoj highlighted with traditional Toran, Shehnai musicians, and Dhol drums.
10. **Family Letter & Child's Plea**: Warm invitation letter featuring the sweet plea by Kunwar Raj Solanki (Ladu) and the awaiting children.
11. **Comprehensive Family Directory**: Preserving all branches: Welcomers (Swagatatur), Hosts (Swagatkarta), Humble Solicitations (Vineet & Entire Gehlot Family), Daughters & Sons-in-law (Javai Paksh), Maternal Family (Nanihal Paksh & Parihar Family), Nephews (Bhanej Paksh), and Special Blessings.
12. **Interactive Contacts & Directions**: Direct Click-to-Call, WhatsApp RSVP, and verified Google Maps navigation link.
13. **Guest Personalization via URL**: Pass `?guest=Sharma+Family` to display a custom guest honor banner.
14. **Print & PDF Layout**: Clean print stylesheet (`print.css`) for high-resolution physical printing and PDF export.
15. **100% Responsive**: Tested across desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile devices with zero horizontal overflow.

## 🚀 Running Locally

To preview locally:

```bash
# Using Python
python -m http.server 3000

# Or using Node.js
npx serve -l 3000
```

Open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.
