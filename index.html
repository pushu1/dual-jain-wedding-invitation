Sacred Traditions & Celebrations

WEDDING EVENTS SCHEDULE

❖

Join us in every joyous milestone and divine ritual. We look forward to celebrating each unforgettable moment with you.

1
Sanctification Ritual 3:15 PM

Bhootpaan (Ghritpaan)

Monday • 7 December 2026

The sacred opening ritual signifying divine purity, ancestral blessings, and the auspicious commencement of the wedding festivities.

2
Divine Invocation 5:15 PM

Vinayak Ceremony

Monday • 7 December 2026

Formal ceremonial invocation of Lord Ganesha to consecrate the home, remove all obstacles, and bless both couples with eternal prosperity.

3
Joy & Henna 1:15 PM

Haldi & Mehendi

Tuesday • 8 December 2026

An exuberant afternoon bathed in radiant golden turmeric paste, delicate bridal henna artistry, traditional folk songs, and joyful laughter.

4
Maternal Blessings 12:15 PM

Mayra Ceremony (Bhat)

Wednesday • 9 December 2026

The time-honored Bhat ceremony where maternal uncles arrive with ceremonial gifts, wedding apparel, and heartfelt familial blessings.

5
Royal Procession 5:15 PM

Bannoli Ceremony

Wednesday • 9 December 2026

Grand traditional royal procession celebrating the bride and groom through town with ceremonial pomp, folk instruments, and sweets.

6
Musical Night 8:00 PM

Ladies Sangeet

Wednesday • 9 December 2026

A dazzling musical evening featuring spirited Rajasthani folk dances, family choreography, melodious singing, and celebratory harmony.

7
Groom Procession 12:15 PM

Baraat Departure

Thursday • 10 December 2026

Ceremonial departure of the groom's procession with beating dholak, brass band melodies, and family rejoicing as the wedding begins.

8
Royal Reception 5:15 PM

Baraat Welcome (Swagat)

Saturday • 12 December 2026

Heartfelt traditional greeting of the baraat with rose petal showers, rosewater fragrance, ceremonial aarti, and joyful welcoming gestures.

9
Sacred Vedic Nuptials 10:15 PM

Wedding Ceremony (Panigrahan Sanskar)

Saturday • 12 December 2026

The holy seven pheras around the consecrated fire uniting both couples in eternal spiritual partnership, love, and sacred companionship.
